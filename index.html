Our story

A quiet room, a serious cup.

9ONE was built as a calm corner of Hargeisa — sculpted arches, warm light, rattan and soft green — where a morning shaah can turn into an afternoon of sheeko.

Behind the bar, we take coffee seriously: espresso pulled to order, hand-poured V60 and lattes finished with care. From the kitchen, everything from suqaar and laxoox at breakfast to slow-cooked goat hanid at night.
